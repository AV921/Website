# Editorial opening image

`editorial-infrastructure-768.webp` and `editorial-infrastructure-1536.webp`
are responsive exports of an AI-generated conceptual architectural image,
created with OpenAI's built-in image generation tool for this redesign.
They do not document a project designed by Andrea Vennarucci.

Art direction: premium architectural editorial photography of a curved
concrete viaduct seen from below, pale sky, textured concrete, distant hills,
warm monochrome silver tones; no people, text, logos or wireframes.

Andrea's portrait remains the original `HeaderAboutMe` image, cropped only
through CSS to fit the new layout.
