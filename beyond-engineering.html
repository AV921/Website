 Poetry collection

La fisica delle cose create is a collection of poems and reflections exploring the relationship between scientific knowledge, existence, uncertainty and the human need to give meaning to reality.

The work brings together the perspective of an engineer and researcher with a more intimate and literary form of observation. Physics becomes not only a discipline, but also a language through which memory, identity, time and creation can be examined.

Perspective

Knowledge is never only technical

My academic and professional path is grounded in engineering, transportation research, digital simulation and road safety. At the same time, I have always considered scientific knowledge part of a broader process of understanding.

Research provides methods for describing phenomena, testing hypotheses and constructing reliable models. Literature and philosophy approach other dimensions of the same reality: the meaning attributed to experience, the limits of language and the way individuals interpret uncertainty and change.

These projects do not replace or compete with my scientific work. They represent another perspective from which I examine the relationship between human beings, knowledge and the world they attempt to understand.
